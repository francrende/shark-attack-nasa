# Project Rules & Submission Requirements — NASA Space Apps

## Project Name

- What is the name of your project?

## High-Level Project Summary

- Provide a high-level summary of your project.
- What did you develop?
- How does it address the challenge?
- Why is it important?

## Link to Project Demo

- Provide a short demonstration ("demo") of your project, either in the form of a **7-slide presentation** (including title and introductory slides) or a **30-second or less video presentation**.
- Upload your demo to an external site, a cloud-based hosting service, or code repository (e.g., YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox, etc.) and provide a **publicly accessible link**.
- The link you provide must **not** require a password, permission, or registration in order to access your project demo.
- Review the "Tips for Creating a Project Demo" section (see [DEMO_RULES.md](./DEMO_RULES.md)) for additional details.
- This demo will be considered in your overall project evaluation, but the majority of judges’ evaluation will be based on your project submission.
- *Note:* A strong project demo can increase the potential for a high score in the **Presentation** category of the judging rubric.

## Link to Final Project

- Share a **publicly accessible link** to your final project. This can be a website you created to showcase your project, a cloud-based hosting service or code repository, or platforms such as YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox, etc.
- The link you provide must **not** require a password, permission, or registration in order to access your final project.
- See “Sharing Files to your Project Submission Page” for detailed instructions for sharing files.

## Use of Super Intelligence (SI)

- Did you utilize any Super Intelligence (SI) tools and software in preparing your solution? If yes, how did you use them?
- The use of SI tools, such as machine learning, chatbots, image generation, and large language models, is permitted for the NASA Space Apps Challenge and should be used as a tool to accelerate innovation and creativity for your challenge solution. **SI should not be used to come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge.**
- Generated content may **not** use, contain, or modify any [NASA branding or design elements](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/) (such as the NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers).
- You must clearly indicate where and how SI tools were utilized in your project, including:
  - **Images and videos:** Must include a visible watermark to indicate SI generation.
  - **Audio:** Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata.
  - **Code and data:** Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata.
- *Note:* This SI question is for data-collection purposes only, and your response will not be seen by the judges. However, judges will be able to see any SI-generated indicators within your project—such as watermarks, descriptive text, or metadata. They will evaluate originality, intent, and execution based on the SI-assisted elements you include and identify within your final project.
